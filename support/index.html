常见检查

  • 捕获需要网络连接，并由你主动运行“谛听捕获”。
  • 日历或提醒事项未保存时，请检查 iOS 设置中的对应系统权限。
  • 识别结果应在保存前由你确认。
  • 新用户获得 100 次免费识别。每次成功捕获最多消耗 1 次；失败识别不扣次数。
  • 现有消耗型商品一次增加 1000 次识别。识别次数保存在设备本地；删除或重新安装 App 后可能无法恢复。
  • 识别次数购买由 Apple App Store 处理。如果购买确认没有出现、交易仍在处理中或余额未更新，请不要连续重复点击购买；记录发生时间、iOS 版本和谛听版本后联系支持。

联系支持时

请提供 iOS 版本、谛听版本、问题发生步骤和大致时间。请勿发送 API Key、Apple 账户密码、付款凭据、收据/JWS，或包含无关私人信息的截图。

Need help? Email malianglai@gmail.com.

Common checks

  • Capture requires a network connection and starts only when you run “谛听捕获”.
  • If a Calendar event or Reminder is not saved, check its permission in iOS Settings.
  • Review each recognition result before saving it.
  • New users receive 100 free recognitions. Each successful capture consumes at most one credit; failed recognition does not consume credits.
  • The existing consumable product adds 1,000 recognitions per purchase. Credits are stored locally and may not be recoverable after deleting or reinstalling the app.
  • Recognition-credit purchases are handled by Apple’s App Store. If the confirmation sheet does not appear, a transaction remains pending, or the balance does not update, do not repeatedly tap Purchase. Note the approximate time, iOS version, and Diting version, then contact support.

When contacting support

Include your iOS version, Diting version, steps to reproduce, and approximate time. Never send an API key, Apple Account password, payment credentials, receipt/JWS, or a screenshot containing unrelated private information.
